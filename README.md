I am a passionate **Full-Stack Software Engineer** and **AI Product Engineer** based in Hanoi, Vietnam. - 🤖 **AI & Automation:** Designing multi-agent workflows (Claude Code, OpenCode, Antigravity) and enterprise RAG pipelines for real-world business domains.
