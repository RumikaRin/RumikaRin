### 👨‍💻 Engineering Profile & Philosophy

I am a **Full-Stack Software Engineer** and **AI Product Engineer** based in Hanoi, Vietnam. My work sits at the intersection of **deep technical architecture**, **AI infrastructure & tooling**, and **refined visual design**. 

Whether building zero-style CSP e-commerce backends, reverse-proxying LLM protocols, constructing procedural motion renderers, or orchestrating multi-agent development pipelines, I hold a strict standard of engineering excellence:

- 🏛️ **Architecture & Security:** Zero-Style CSP with per-request dynamic nonces, RFC 6238 TOTP MFA, Database Session Registries with instant revocation, and atomic concurrency ledgers that eliminate race conditions and deadlocks.
- 🤖 **AI Infrastructure & Tooling:** Building custom OpenAI-compatible reverse-proxy adapters with account pooling and automatic 429 failover, enterprise RAG knowledge pipelines, and multi-agent orchestration systems (Claude Code, OpenCode, Antigravity).
- 🎨 **Creative Engineering:** Deterministic pure-function video generation engines (`window.seek(t)`), interactive 3D Spline experiences, and smooth momentum kinematics.
- 🧪 **Verification Without Compromise:** Test-Driven Development (TDD) with multi-tiered quality gates (Unit, PostgreSQL Docker Integration, OpenAPI 3.1 contracts, WCAG contrast audits, and Playwright E2E browser testing).
